📊 Elo Rating System

Each team has an Elo rating that updates based on game results. Home court adds ~100 Elo points (roughly 3-4% win probability). Playoff experience and historical Finals success are factored in.

Offensive and defensive efficiency ratings, pace, shooting percentages, and rebounding rates are combined into a composite strength score. Weighted toward recent performance.

10,000 simulations of the remaining series, each using the per-game probabilities. Accounts for series dynamics like desperation, momentum, and close-out pressure.
